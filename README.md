# Deep Drop PRO — Tournament Edition

Championship water for the single-file browser fishing game. Same boat, same hook, far sharper fish.

Looking for the standard edition? It's at [rebeccafarrell/deep-drop](https://github.com/rebeccafarrell/deep-drop).

## How to play

| Action | Control |
| --- | --- |
| Steer the boat | `←` `→` (or move the mouse) |
| Drop the hook | `Space` or click |

## What's different in Pro

Tournament fish have seen every hook in the harbour. They read your line early, break away from its path, and slide vertically out of reach rather than swimming into it. Expect to work for every point.

Your end-of-round card tracks **casts** and **near misses** alongside your score, so you can measure exactly how close you came.
